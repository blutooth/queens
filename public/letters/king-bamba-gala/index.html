19th August 2026

His Majesty King Guide Bamba El Hadj Mahamed Saanwi

King of Mali

Re:  Invitation to Speak — African Global Queens Summit 2026, United Kingdom

Your Majesty,

On behalf of the African Global Queens Summit Organising Committee, and in my own name as Convener, it is with profound honour, the deepest respect and immense admiration that I extend to Your Majesty this special invitation to grace the African Global Queens Summit 2026, to be held across London and Oxford, United Kingdom, from 14 to 31 August 2026, and to address our distinguished gathering as a valued and honoured Speaker.

The African Global Queens Summit is an esteemed international gathering that celebrates visionary leadership, cultural heritage, social transformation and excellence among distinguished leaders from Africa and the global diaspora. It seeks to foster meaningful dialogue, strengthen international partnerships and inspire future generations through the wisdom and accomplishments of exceptional individuals whose lives exemplify service, dignity and impact.

As His Majesty King Guide Bamba El Hadj Mahamed Saanwi, King of Mali, Your Majesty stands among the most revered custodians of Africa’s royal heritage — a sovereign whose leadership embodies peace, unity, cultural stewardship and the enduring dignity of our traditional institutions. It is precisely this eminence and inspiring example that we are honoured to place before the sovereigns, traditional leaders and delegates gathered for this landmark occasion.

Accordingly, it is our privilege to invite Your Majesty to speak as a valued Speaker at the Summit’s flagship celebration:

  • The African Global Queens Gala Night — Porchester Hall, London, 23 August 2026 — an evening of recognition, culture and the arts, and a fitting stage for Your Majesty’s voice and vision.

Your Majesty’s address would greatly enrich this occasion and provide invaluable inspiration to a distinguished audience comprising government officials, diplomats, traditional rulers, business leaders, academics, faith leaders, philanthropists and emerging leaders from around the world.

We sincerely hope that Your Majesty will honour us by accepting this invitation. Upon your gracious acceptance, the Secretariat will make every provision for Your Majesty’s participation, hospitality and comfort throughout your engagements, and will liaise with your office on all matters of protocol and programme.

Please accept, Your Majesty, the assurances of our highest esteem, profound respect and warmest fraternal regard.

Yours faithfully,

Obonganwan Barr. Marie Ikpeme Erete
