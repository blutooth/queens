African Global Queens Summit

Statement of Condolences

Passing of His Majesty King Oyo Nyimba Kabamba Iguru Rukidi IV, Omukama of the Kingdom of Toro

It is with great sadness that the African Global Queens Summit has learned of the passing of His Majesty King Oyo Nyimba Kabamba Iguru Rukidi IV, Omukama of the Kingdom of Toro, in Uganda.

The African Global Queens Summit honours the memory of a monarch who devoted himself to the prosperity of his kingdom and who showed a deep sense of responsibility since his accession to the throne. The Kingdom of Toro is among the great custodial institutions of our continent, and he understood what had been placed into his hands.

He belonged to a generation of African monarchs who hold ancient offices in a modern age, and who bear the double burden of honouring what was handed down while speaking to a world that has changed around it. He carried that burden with dignity. That he should be taken so young is a loss felt far beyond the borders of his kingdom.

To Her Majesty Queen Best Kemigisa

Her Majesty is not a stranger to us. She is a sister by the oldest bond we know — that of African queenship itself — and what wounds the Royal House of Toro is felt by every throne on this continent. No register records that kinship and no ceremony confers it. It is simply so, and on a day such as this it asks something of us.

Your Majesty, there is no title that softens this and no crown that lightens it. A mother’s grief is its own country, and we do not pretend to know its language. But you do not walk in it alone. Across borders and across oceans, the Queens of Africa and of her Diaspora hold Your Majesty in prayer and in the highest respect. Should the day come when Your Majesty wishes to walk more closely with this Summit, our doors stand open. Until then, and always, you are our sister — and you are not alone.

We hold you in our prayers and in our deepest affection. Whatever comfort a family of nations can offer is yours, for as long as you have need of it. You are not alone.

To Her Royal Highness Princess Ruth Komuntale, who has lost a brother, we extend our tenderest sympathy. To the elders, the chiefs and the people of the Kingdom of Toro, who have lost a father to their nation, our hearts go out to you. And to the entire Ugandan nation, we send our condolences and our deepest respect in this hour of national loss.

The African Global Queens Summit stands with the Royal House of Toro today, and will stand with it through the days ahead. May the throne he leaves be steadied by the love of his people, and may his memory be kept as carefully as he kept his kingdom.

May the King’s soul rest in peace.

Long live African unity and brotherhood!

For the African Global Queens Summit

Prince Iganda Kamole
