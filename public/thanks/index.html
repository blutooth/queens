A Message of Gratitude & Special Appreciation

African & Diaspora Queens Summit

Oxford & London · 2026

✦ ◆ ✦

With a heart filled with profound gratitude, humility and immense pride, I wish to extend my deepest appreciation to everyone who attended, supported, contributed to and helped make the African & Diaspora Queens Summit 2026 in Oxford and London a memorable and meaningful gathering.

What began as a vision became a beautiful convergence of African royalty, traditional leadership, dignitaries, women leaders, scholars, cultural ambassadors, artists, young people and friends of Africa and the Diaspora.

Your presence gave life to our vision. Your voices enriched our conversations. Your cultures strengthened our fellowship. And your contributions reminded us that, despite the distances that may separate us, our heritage, our humanity and our aspirations unite us.

The Summit Has Ended — But the Journey Continues

The curtains may have closed on the African & Diaspora Queens Summit 2026, but the vision that brought us together must continue.

We must transform conversations into partnerships, partnerships into programmes, and programmes into lasting opportunities for our people.

  • We must continue to champion the dignity of African heritage.
  • We must empower African women.
  • We must invest in our young people.
  • We must preserve our languages, traditions and cultures.
  • We must encourage education, entrepreneurship, creativity and responsible leadership.

And above all, we must teach the next generation that being African is not something to apologise for — it is something to understand, protect, celebrate and build upon.

Our young people must not feel compelled to abandon their identity in order to succeed in the world. They must be confident enough to embrace the best of Africa while engaging intelligently with the wider world.

Let us raise a generation that does not merely consume culture, but creates culture; that does not merely follow trends, but sets them; that does not merely seek opportunities abroad, but creates opportunities at home; and that understands that Africa's greatest resource is not beneath the soil — it is within her people.

A Final Word of Gratitude

To everyone who stood with us, believed in the vision and contributed to the success of this Summit, I say from the depths of my heart:

Thank you. Thank you. Thank you.

May the friendships formed in Oxford and London endure. May the partnerships created during these days flourish. May the conversations we began continue across borders. May the young people we inspired become the leaders, innovators and builders of a better Africa.

And may the African & Diaspora Queens Summit become not merely an annual gathering, but a lasting institution of African unity, cultural preservation, female leadership, youth empowerment and global engagement.

We came together from different nations, different traditions and different backgrounds. But we leave with one shared understanding:

Africa is one family.

The Diaspora is part of that family.

And together, our possibilities are limitless.
