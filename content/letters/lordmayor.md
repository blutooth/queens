**Re: Invitation to the Royal Gala Night — Fashion, Art & Entertainment — Tuesday, 25 August 2026, Porchester Hall, London**

On behalf of the African Global Queens Summit Organising Committee, and with the highest esteem, it is my privilege to invite you — **together with the Lady Mayoress and your family** — to the Royal Gala Night of the **African Global Queens Summit 2026**.

## The Occasion

- **Date** — Tuesday, 25 August 2026
- **Venue** — Porchester Hall
- **Address** — Porchester Road, Bayswater, London, W2 5HS
- **Dress** — Black tie · Traditional royal attire warmly welcomed

The Gala is the flagship evening of the Summit — an evening of fashion, art and entertainment gathering reigning African Queens, Queen Mothers, Kings, traditional rulers, heads of mission and distinguished guests from across the continent and the Diaspora, held in the gilded Edwardian ballroom of Porchester Hall in the heart of London.

## An Invitation Extended to Your Family

Your Worship's courtesy to the Summit at Oxford on 20 August was received with the warmest appreciation by our delegation. It is therefore our particular wish that this invitation extend beyond your office to your household: **the Lady Mayoress and your family are most cordially invited to accompany you** as guests of honour for the evening, and places will be reserved for them alongside your own.

So that the Secretariat may reserve the correct number of seats and prepare the place settings, we should be grateful to receive the names of those who will accompany you at your earliest convenience.

## With Our Respects

Right Worshipful Lord Mayor, your presence — and that of the Lady Mayoress and your family — would honour the Summit, the City of Oxford, and the many royal houses gathered under our roof this evening.

I look forward to welcoming you all personally.
