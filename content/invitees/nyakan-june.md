Your Excellency,

**Re: Invitation to Address the African Global Queens Summit 2026, and Visa Support**

It is with the greatest admiration and respect that I extend this personal invitation to Your Excellency to join us as a distinguished **Guest Speaker** at the **African Global Queens Summit 2026**, to be convened in London and Oxford, United Kingdom, from **14 to 31 August 2026**.

## A Voice of Distinction

As **Global Chief Executive Officer of Timeless Women of Wonder Africa** and **Host of Accelerate Africa's Growth Connect**, Your Excellency has become a leading voice for the empowerment of women and the advancement of Africa's growth and enterprise. Your leadership, vision, and unwavering dedication to uplifting women across the continent and the Diaspora embody the very spirit this Summit seeks to advance.

## A Trailblazer and Presidential Aspirant

Your Excellency has also stepped courageously onto the national stage as a **Presidential aspirant** in Kenya — a bold undertaking in an arena where women who aspire to the highest office continue to face formidable barriers of access, resources, and long-entrenched bias. Undaunted by these challenges, you have built platforms that mentor women in enterprise and leadership, convened dialogue on Africa's growth, and given voice to the aspirations of countless women — proving that the path to leadership at every level, however difficult, can and must be walked. It is precisely this courage and perseverance that our Summit seeks to celebrate and amplify.

## An Invitation to Speak

It would be our profound honour to welcome Your Excellency to the podium, to share your wisdom and inspiration with the African Queens, Queen Mothers, traditional leaders, dignitaries, and changemakers gathered from across the continent and the Diaspora. Your address would enrich our proceedings and inspire all who have the privilege of attending.
