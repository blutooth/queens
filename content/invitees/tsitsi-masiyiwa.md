Dear Dr Masiyiwa,

It is with the greatest admiration and respect that I extend this personal invitation to you to attend the **African Global Queens Summit 2026**, to be convened in London and Oxford, United Kingdom, from **14 to 31 August 2026**.

## A Champion of Education and Compassion

As **Co-founder and Executive Chair of the Higherlife Foundation**, you have transformed the lives of tens of thousands of orphaned and vulnerable children across Zimbabwe, Burundi and Lesotho through education and opportunity. Through **Delta Philanthropies**, and as a co-founder of the **END Fund** and of **Co-Impact**, your leadership in strategic philanthropy has advanced health, education, and women's empowerment across the continent and beyond — earning you recognition among the most influential philanthropists of our time.

## An Invitation of Honour

Your presence would lend immeasurable distinction to this gathering of African Queens, traditional leaders, and changemakers from across the continent and the Diaspora. It would be our profound honour to welcome a daughter of **Zimbabwe** whose life so wholly embodies the Summit's spirit — *leadership rooted in service*.

## Duration &amp; Logistics

Guests are respectfully requested to reserve the full period **14–31 August 2026** to enable meaningful participation across all Summit engagements. The Secretariat will be glad to assist with arrangements following your kind confirmation.

Dr Masiyiwa, your voice and your example would inspire all who gather. I look forward to welcoming you personally to the United Kingdom.

Please accept the assurances of my highest esteem and warmest regard.
