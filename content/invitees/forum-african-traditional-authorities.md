Your Majesties, Your Royal Highnesses, and Distinguished Rulers,

**Re: A Royal Invitation to the African Global Queens Summit 2026**

It is with the deepest respect and warm fraternal regard that I extend this invitation to the **traditional rulers of Africa** — the Kings, Queens, Queen Mothers, Sultans, Chiefs, and custodians of custom drawn from across every region of our continent — to grace the **African Global Queens Summit 2026**, to be convened in London and Oxford, United Kingdom, from **14 to 31 August 2026**.

## A Convocation of Traditional Africa

Africa's traditional rulers stand as the guardians of our heritage — those who hold, in trust for generations yet unborn, the customs, wisdom, and dignity of our continent's ancient institutions. It is precisely this spirit that the African Global Queens Summit seeks to honour: a gathering that unites reigning royals, traditional leaders, scholars, and changemakers from the continent and the Diaspora in a shared celebration of culture, leadership, and unity.

## An Invitation to the Rulers of Africa

We warmly invite the traditional rulers of Africa to take their rightful place among this historic gathering — to lend their voice, their counsel, and their presence to a collective vision for the advancement of our peoples. Your participation would affirm the enduring authority of Africa's traditional institutions upon the global stage.

## The Honour of Your Participation

Their Majesties and Royal Highnesses are respectfully requested to reserve the full period **14–31 August 2026** to ensure meaningful participation across all Summit engagements — from the Ceremonial Opening and the University of Oxford Leadership Programme to the African Global Queens Gala Night and the closing celebrations. The Secretariat will be glad to assist with the arrangements for your delegation following confirmation of participation.

Your Majesties, Your Royal Highnesses, your presence would lend immeasurable distinction to this landmark occasion. It would be our profound honour to welcome you to the United Kingdom.

Please accept the assurances of my highest esteem and warmest regard.
