The Right Honourable The Lord Stanley of Alderley\
Lady Stanley and the Stanley Family\
Rectory Farm, Stanton St John\
Oxfordshire

Your Lordship, Lady Stanley, and Members of the Stanley Family,

It is with profound respect and the greatest personal pleasure that I extend this invitation to Your Lordship, Lady Stanley, and your distinguished family to be our honoured guests at the **African Global Queens Summit**, to be convened in London and Oxford from **14 to 31 August 2026**.

This Summit marks an historic occasion, bringing together reigning African Queens, Queen Mothers, Royal Consorts, Traditional Rulers, eminent leaders, scholars, diplomats, and distinguished guests from around the world in a celebration of cultural heritage, enlightened leadership, and international friendship. It seeks to strengthen the bonds between peoples through mutual respect, dialogue, education, and a shared commitment to preserving the traditions that enrich our nations.

As part of this landmark gathering, Oxfordshire will welcome Royal Houses from across the African continent and the diaspora. It is therefore especially fitting to acknowledge the longstanding contribution of the Stanley family to the life of Stanton St John and to the preservation of England's historic rural heritage.

Across the centuries, your family's devotion to public service, stewardship of the land, and commitment to community have earned enduring respect. These are values deeply cherished by the traditional institutions represented at our Summit, where Queens continue to serve not merely as ceremonial figures but as guardians of culture, custodians of history, and advocates for the well-being and advancement of their people.

It would be a singular privilege to welcome Your Lordship, Lady Stanley, and members of your family to this unprecedented gathering. Your presence would stand as a gracious affirmation of the enduring importance of heritage, tradition, and the friendships that flourish when communities from different cultures meet in a spirit of goodwill and mutual esteem.

We would be particularly honoured by your attendance at our **Ceremonial Opening**, the **Lord Mayor's Dinner**, and the **African Global Queens Gala Evening**, where representatives of governments, diplomatic missions, universities, business, philanthropy, and traditional institutions will join together in celebration of this remarkable occasion.

May I take this opportunity to express my sincere admiration for the Stanley family's enduring legacy and your continuing contribution to the community of Stanton St John. It would be a privilege beyond measure to receive you as our esteemed guests and to welcome you personally.

Please accept, Your Lordship, Lady Stanley, and the Stanley family, the assurances of my highest esteem, profound respect, and warmest good wishes.
