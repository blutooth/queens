Your Excellency,

**Re: Formal Invitation to the African Global Queens Summit 2026, and Invitation to Represent His Excellency the President of the Republic of Equatorial Guinea**

On behalf of the African Global Queens Summit Organising Committee, and in accordance with established diplomatic protocol, it is with the highest esteem and the deepest respect that I extend this formal invitation to Your Excellency to attend the **African Global Queens Summit 2026**, to be convened in London and Oxford, United Kingdom, from **14 to 31 August 2026**.

## A Gathering of Historic Distinction

The Summit is an esteemed international convocation uniting reigning African Queens, Queen Mothers, Kings, traditional rulers, heads of mission, government representatives, scholars, and distinguished guests from across the continent and the Diaspora. It is convened to celebrate our shared heritage, to strengthen royal and diplomatic ties between Africa and the United Kingdom, and to advance the cultural, economic, and moral presence of Africa upon the global stage.

## An Invitation to Represent the Head of State

It would be our profound and singular honour for Your Excellency to attend and, **on behalf of His Excellency the President of the Republic of Equatorial Guinea**, to represent the Head of State and the people of your nation at this historic gathering, with all the ceremonial honour and distinction that so eminent a mandate deserves. Your Excellency would be received with full protocol and accorded a place of honour among the sovereigns and heads of mission in attendance.

## Engagements of Honour

We would be especially honoured by Your Excellency's presence at the **Ceremonial Opening**, the **Lord Mayor's Dinner**, and the **African Global Queens Gala Evening**, where representatives of governments, diplomatic missions, universities, and traditional institutions will gather in celebration of this remarkable occasion.

## Logistics

The Secretariat stands ready to extend every courtesy — protocol, scheduling, and liaison — to Your Excellency and any accompanying delegation, and will be glad to make the necessary arrangements following your kind confirmation.

Your Excellency, the presence of the Republic of Equatorial Guinea, borne in your distinguished person, would lend immeasurable dignity to this landmark occasion.

Please accept, Your Excellency, the assurances of my highest consideration and most distinguished regard.
