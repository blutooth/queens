Dear Chief Alakija,

It is with the greatest admiration and respect that I extend this personal invitation to you to attend the **African Global Queens Summit 2026**, to be convened in London and Oxford, United Kingdom, from **14 to 31 August 2026**.

## A Titan of Enterprise and Philanthropy

One of Africa's foremost businesswomen, you rose from the world of fashion — as founder of the **Rose of Sharon House of Fashion** — to become **Executive Vice-Chairman of Famfa Oil** and **Group Managing Director of The Rose of Sharon Group**, building an enterprise that has placed you among the most successful and respected women on the continent. Through the **Rose of Sharon Foundation**, you have restored hope to countless widows and orphans, and as an author, mentor, and former University Chancellor, you have devoted your success to the upliftment of others.

## An Invitation of Honour

Your presence would lend immeasurable distinction to this gathering of African Queens, traditional leaders, and changemakers from across the continent and the Diaspora. It would be our profound honour to welcome a daughter of **Nigeria** whose life so wholly embodies enterprise, faith, and generosity of spirit.

## Duration & Logistics

Guests are respectfully requested to reserve the full period **14–31 August 2026** to enable meaningful participation across all Summit engagements. The Secretariat will be glad to assist with arrangements following your kind confirmation.

Chief Alakija, your voice and your example would inspire all who gather. I look forward to welcoming you personally to the United Kingdom.

Please accept the assurances of my highest esteem and warmest regard.
