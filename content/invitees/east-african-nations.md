Your Excellencies,

**Re: A Royal Invitation to the African Global Queens Summit 2026**

It is with profound respect and warm fraternal regard that I extend this invitation to the sovereign nations and peoples of **East Africa** — Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, Somalia, Djibouti, Ethiopia, Eritrea, and the Democratic Republic of Congo — to grace the **African Global Queens Summit 2026**, to be convened in London and Oxford, United Kingdom, from **14 to 31 August 2026**.

## A Gathering of the Continent

The African Global Queens Summit is an esteemed international assembly that unites reigning African Queens, Queen Mothers, Royal Consorts, Traditional Rulers, government leaders, scholars, and distinguished changemakers from across the continent and the Diaspora. It is convened to celebrate our shared heritage, to affirm the enduring dignity of our institutions, and to advance Africa's cultural, economic, and moral presence within the global order.

## An Invitation to East Africa

The nations of East Africa have long stood as custodians of ancient kingdoms, guardians of rich cultural heritage, and pillars of the continent's unity and progress. We therefore warmly invite the **Queens, Queen Mothers, Kings, traditional rulers, royal houses, government representatives, and distinguished delegations** of East Africa to take their rightful place among this historic gathering, and to lend their voice to a shared vision for the advancement of our peoples.

## The Honour of Your Participation

Delegations are respectfully requested to reserve the full period **14–31 August 2026** to ensure meaningful participation across all Summit engagements — from the Ceremonial Opening and the University of Oxford Leadership Programme to the African Global Queens Gala Night and the closing celebrations. The Secretariat will be glad to assist with the arrangements for your delegation following confirmation of participation.

Your Excellencies, the presence of East Africa would lend immeasurable distinction to this landmark occasion. It would be our profound honour to welcome your royal houses and representatives to the United Kingdom.

Please accept, Your Excellencies, the assurances of my highest esteem and warmest regard.
