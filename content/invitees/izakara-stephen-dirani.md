Your Royal Highness,

**Re: A Special Invitation to Address the African Global Queens Summit 2026**

It is with the highest esteem and fraternal regard that I extend this special invitation to Your Royal Highness to attend and to address the **African Global Queens Summit 2026**, to be convened in London and Oxford, United Kingdom, from **14 to 31 August 2026**.

## A Distinguished Traditional Leader

As **Paramount Chief of the Madi Peoples** and **President General of the Forum of African Traditional Authorities (FATA)** — the body that unites the traditional authorities of every territory and province across our continent — Your Royal Highness stands among the foremost custodians of Africa's heritage, and a tireless champion of the dignity and unity of our institutions.

## An Invitation to Speak

It would be our profound honour to welcome Your Royal Highness to the podium as a distinguished **Guest Speaker**, to share your wisdom on traditional leadership, cultural heritage, and African unity with the Queens, Queen Mothers, traditional rulers, and delegates gathered from across the continent and the Diaspora. Your voice would enrich our proceedings and inspire all who attend.

## Self-Sponsored Participation

We gratefully acknowledge that Your Royal Highness will attend the Summit on a **self-sponsored basis**, meeting the cost of your own travel, accommodation, and participation. The Secretariat will nonetheless be delighted to extend every courtesy and assistance — protocol, scheduling, and logistical support — to ensure your visit is seamless and befitting your esteemed office.

## Duration & Logistics

Your Royal Highness is respectfully requested to reserve the full period **14–31 August 2026** to enable meaningful participation across all Summit engagements. The Secretariat remains at your service for any arrangements following your kind confirmation.

Your Royal Highness, your presence and your voice would lend immeasurable distinction to this historic gathering. I look forward to welcoming you personally to the United Kingdom.

Please accept, Your Royal Highness, the assurances of my highest esteem and warmest regard.
